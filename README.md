1. Adventure Works Report: An in depth analysis for a biking store

The Adventure Works Report is a visually engaging and analytical Power BI report designed to help users explore various KPIs of a fictional bike store like returns, sales, most selling product, least in demand products etc. The dashboard focuses on highlighting the major areas of the business and making it easier to understand with visuals.

Tools Used for Building the Power BI Report were:-
->Power BI – Used to create the interactive report, dashboards, visualizations, and overall presentation.
->Power Query – Used for importing, cleaning, transforming, and preparing the raw data.
->DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used to create calculated measures, conditional formatting and perform data analysis.
->Data Modeling – Used to establish relationships between tables and organize the data for efficient reporting.
->File Formats – Used CSV files as the primary source format for importing transaction data into Power BI.

Data Source: The data was taken in from Udemy after the completion of my course, where we had to build a dashboard ourselves based on the data they gave.

Features/Highlights:
->Clearly defined goals
->Track how the business is doing 
->Various diagrams for easier understanding

Business Problem: The revenue per customer was seen going down drastically from August of 2021.

Business Insgights:
The business can use this dashboard to figure out where they are doing well, where they need to improve and also categorize their customers, giving priority and special offers to those who purchase from us very often.
